# Pesto

## Ingredients
- Basil (around 3 plants)
- Olive oil
- Garlic (around 4-6 cloves)
- Pine seeds (One package)
- Parmesan
- Pecorino
- Salt

## Instructions
- Remove basil leaves from stems and wash with water and dry off
- Add to blender and blend until desired consistency

## Notes
- Amount of ingredients depends on preference. There should be enough olive oil to keep it oily.
- Do not blend too long as basil turns bitter if blended too much
